# Rift24 - thesis and findings

> **Paper/backtest only. No live-money trading.** Generated by `python -m scripts.run_backtest`; every number below is read from the result objects, none is typed by hand. Labels: **OBSERVED** = read from exchange/cash data, **ESTIMATED** = depends on a modelled assumption (costs, spread, net-of-cost returns, implied gaps), **TARGETED** = not yet built or measured.

## 1. The question

U.S. cash equities close at 16:00 ET and reopen at 09:30 ET - 17.5 hours overnight and about 65 hours over a weekend. Bitget's rTokens trade 24/7 in that gap (the *void*). Information arrives during it, so the rToken prints a **void move** before the cash market has printed anything. Rift24 asks one thing: **how much of the eventual cash-market gap has the rToken already priced by the time we would have to act - and is the remainder bigger than the cost of trading it?**

*Pre-specified hypothesis H1 (from the brief):* rTokens over-move in the void, so **fading** large void moves (`signal = -R_rtoken`) earns positive risk-adjusted returns after costs.

## 2. Headline

1. **H1 is rejected** (OBSERVED prices, ESTIMATED costs). At the pre-specified 09:15 ET decision time the void move is essentially fully priced: the cash gap equals **1.03x** the void move (95% CI [1.01, 1.05], n=497 large moves; OOS 1.01 [0.97, 1.05]; weekends only 0.98 [0.93, 1.04]). Beta at or above 1 means no over-shoot to fade.
2. **Always-fade loses** 15.7 bps per trade net (Sharpe -14.26); before costs it earns -3.7 bps (t=-1.97), i.e. slightly negative, not positive - most of the loss is the 12 bps cost.
3. **Rift24 stands down on 100.0% of symbol-sessions** (0 trades of 620): with no over-shoot and a 12 bps cost, the gate never finds an edge worth twice the cost. It refused 497 trades that always-fade took, which averaged -15.7 bps net - here, doing nothing was the better trade.
4. **The residual runs the other way earlier in the void (exploratory, post-hoc).** Deciding at 04:00 ET instead of 09:15, the cash gap is larger than the void move (beta up to 1.17): rTokens *under*-react to big overnight gaps. A two-sided residual rule made 47 trades (OOS Sharpe 5.22 ± 3.64). **This was found after the null result, on the same sample - treat it as a hypothesis, not an edge** (section 6).

## 3. How a session is built (no look-ahead)

```
prior cash close -- rToken trades through the void -- DECISION -- entry ---------- exit -- cash OPEN
16:00 ET (base price)                                 09:15 ET*   next bar   last pre-open bar   09:30
                                                       (* pre-specified; the exploratory study moves the decision earlier)
```

- rToken = Bitget spot 15-minute candles (OBSERVED). Cash = Yahoo Finance daily regular-session open/close (OBSERVED). `R_rtoken` = rToken price at the decision time / rToken price at the prior cash close - 1. `R_cash` = cash open / prior cash close - 1.
- The decision function receives a `PreOpenView` that has no cash-open, entry or exit field. The priced-in model is refit **walk-forward** on sessions whose exit bar had already closed before the decision. `tests/test_no_lookahead.py` rewrites every post-decision input and asserts no decision changes.
- **Decisions, entries and exits all happen while cash is closed**: the exit is the close of the last 15-minute bar before the open, so no position is ever opened or closed in the regular session. Sessions come from a calendar with holidays, early closes and DST, cross-checked against the days Yahoo has bars for.
- **Design correction (disclosed):** the first draft exited 15 minutes *after* the open. Inspecting the weekend replay showed that bar carried regular-session price action (e.g. rMETA +348 bps in the first bar after the open on 21 Sep), which inflated the exploratory results and broke gate 1 in spirit. Exits were moved before the open, everything was recomputed, and the old exit is kept as a sensitivity row. The pre-specified H1 verdict did not change; the exploratory edge roughly halved out-of-sample.
- Window **2026-06-24 -> 2026-09-21** (90 calendar days, 62 sessions, 13 weekend voids, 10 rTokens): **IN-SAMPLE 2026-06-24 -> 2026-08-22** (60 d), **OUT-OF-SAMPLE 2026-08-23 -> 2026-09-21** (30 d). Data validation (OBSERVED): the rToken's first trade in the 09:30 bar equals Yahoo's cash open to a median of 1.92 bps (87.9% of 620 sessions within 10 bps) - two unrelated sources agreeing, so the session alignment and the cash series are sound. Mechanics: regressing the rToken's realised pre-open move on (R_cash - R_void) gives slope 0.827.

## 5. What is actually priced in

`beta` regresses the realised cash gap on the void move, through the origin, over large moves (|R| >= 2x cost). beta < 1: the rToken over-shot (fade thesis). beta > 1: it under-shot. **OBSERVED.**

| sample | beta | 95% CI | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| ALL | 1.03 | [1.01, 1.05] | 497 |
| IS | 1.04 | [1.01, 1.06] | 340 |
| OOS | 1.01 | [0.97, 1.05] | 157 |
| weekend | 0.98 | [0.93, 1.04] | 110 |
| overnight | 1.04 | [1.01, 1.06] | 387 |

Large void moves over-shot the cash gap 48.5% of the time, and by more than the cost 32.6% of the time - close to a coin flip with fat tails on both sides, not a bias. Median priced-in ratio `R_rtoken / R_cash` = 0.972.

- OOS/IS Sharpe ratio (residual): 0.76 - OOS Sharpe >= 0.5 x IS Sharpe.
- **Concentration:** 47 trades on only 21 distinct days (day-level mean 116.2 bps, t = 3.62). The best 3 days (2026-07-30, 2026-07-23, 2026-08-25) are 45% of P&L; without them the strategy still returns 4.217% (Sharpe 5.495), and 81% of trading days are winners.
- **What the trades are:** large overnight gaps (median |void| 217 bps; the pattern looks like earnings-type gap days) that the thin overnight rToken book only partly prints; it keeps repricing toward the cash open before 09:30. The naive always-follow earns 22.1 bps net per trade at this hour; the residual gate concentrates it.
- **Cost stress:** fees 20 bps + spread 4 bps: 15 trades (2 OOS), IS Sharpe 5.39, OOS Sharpe 3.65; fees 8 bps + thin-book spread 25 bps: 11 trades (2 OOS), IS Sharpe 4.56, OOS Sharpe 3.41; fees 20 bps + thin-book spread 25 bps: 7 trades (1 OOS), IS Sharpe 2.74, OOS Sharpe -3.55. Harsher costs shrink the trade count to a handful - fewer trades rather than a collapse in quality, but far too few to conclude anything.
- **Side:** BUY 27 trades (mean 67.04 bps), SELL/SHORT 20 trades (mean 100.31 bps). Spot rTokens cannot be shorted, so the short half needs a short-capable venue.

**Why this is not a claim of edge:** it was chosen after the pre-specified test failed, on the same ~90 days and ~13 weekends; the decision time was picked from a 5-point grid (by in-sample Sharpe, but the author had already seen OOS numbers); the sample is concentrated on earnings-type days; the OOS Sharpe standard error is as large as the estimate; and a 04:00 ET entry into a thin book is not verified executable at this size. What survives is a specific, falsifiable hypothesis for a forward test.

## 7. Why refusing to trade is the strategy

Rift24 chooses among ADD, FADE and DO NOTHING using a signed residual: `residual = model-implied cash gap - move already printed`, judged against cost. Binding gate at the pre-specified time: Gate 3 123, Gate 5 497 (of 620 symbol-sessions). The gates are transparent: Gate 3 needs |move| >= 2x cost; Gate 5 needs an estimated edge >= 2x cost with statistical support from **past** sessions only (30+ observations); with fewer, the answer is DO NOTHING, not a guess. Stand-down is counted in every metric: sessions without a trade contribute 0, and the stand-down rate is reported next to Sharpe.

## 8. Costs and assumptions

- Round-trip taker-style haircut **8 bps** (ESTIMATED, default from the brief; observed exchange fee is 20 bps round trip). Spread haircut **4 bps** - ESTIMATED fallback: historical bid/ask is not public, so no backtest spread is presented as observed. Extra slippage: 0. Total 12 bps per round trip.
- Sizing: equal weight over the day's trades, capped at 20% per name, gross <= 100% (unlevered). Fills at the next 15-minute bar's open (entry) and the close of the last pre-open bar (exit).
- Missing data: 15-minute bars with no trades are absent (mostly weekends). Prices carry forward at most 4 bars; entry/exit bars must contain a trade or the session is excluded for every strategy. Excluded in window: none.
- Data snapshot: OBSERVED: Bitget spot 15m candles + Yahoo Finance daily open/close, fetched 2026-09-21T21:31:52.338010+00:00.

## 9. Limitations, and what would change the conclusion

- ~90 days and ~13 weekends is a short, single-regime sample; rTokens listed on 2026-06-01. Nothing here says the null holds in other regimes.
- The void is measured against the *regular session*. U.S. pre-market (from 04:00 ET) and after-hours venues already price information, and Bitget states rToken prices are linked to U.S. exchange liquidity - consistent with beta ~ 1 by 09:15 ET. The interesting residual, if any, lives before pre-market liquidity arrives.
- Spread is assumed, not observed. Bitget-reported rToken volumes are implausibly large relative to the underlying cash markets, so volume is not used as evidence of liquidity.
- Ex-dividend gaps in the cash series are not adjusted (negligible over this window). The FADE/SELL leg needs a short-capable venue.
- **What would change the conclusion:** a forward test of the 04:00 ET residual rule on untouched data (TARGETED); observed after-hours spreads and depth (TARGETED); and more weekends.
