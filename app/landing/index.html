The void isn't empty

When cash closes, information doesn't.

Bitget's rTokens — tokenized, 1:1-backed versions of U.S. stocks — trade 24 / 7. In the gap between one cash close and the next cash open, news can arrive and move the rToken before the cash market has printed anything at all.

01

Cash close

16:00 ET (13:00 on early closes). The U.S. regular session ends and prints nothing more until the next open.

02

The void

News and market information keep arriving. rNVDA, rTSLA, rQQQ and the rest of the allow-list keep printing 24 / 7 on Bitget.

03

Cash open

09:30 ET. The traditional market reopens, and Rift24 can compare what the rToken had already priced against what actually happened.

How Rift24 works

Price the void.

A deterministic, gated engine — no discretion, no news feed to interpret, nothing to believe.

01
Observe

Capture the rToken's move during the cash-market closure — the void move, measured directly from the tape.

02

03

Estimate what portion of the eventual cash-market gap already appears reflected in the void move, net of cost.

04

Who this is for

Crypto-native Bitget retail / lower-VIP traders with roughly $3k–$25k capital who hold 2–10 liquid rTokens and sleep through the U.S. cash session — not a professional stat-arb desk.

What stops it from guessing

  • Cash market must be closed at decision time
  • Only allow-listed rTokens — no ad-hoc tickers
  • Move must exceed 2× round-trip cost
  • Costs and spread are priced in, never assumed away
  • Edge must be established from past sessions only, or it's DO NOTHING
  • Stale data (>15 min) forces DO NOTHING in live mode

Data integrity

Every figure is labelled.

OBSERVED

Read directly from exchange / cash-market data — Bitget 15-minute candles, Yahoo Finance daily bars.

ESTIMATED

Depends on a modelled assumption — costs, spread haircut, implied cash gap, net-of-cost returns.

TARGETED

A goal that is not yet built or measured — e.g. a live Bitget Signal feed, or a forward test of the exploratory rule.
